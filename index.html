The promise

When pain asks “Why?”, discover where to place your trust.

Fear asks questions.
Scripture gives us somewhere to stand.

Over 31 days, this devotional guides readers through Scripture, faith statements, prayer, and reflection — helping confront fear, renew the mind, understand God's character, and develop confidence in His healing promises.

This is not a medical promise or a formula for healing. It is an invitation to know God more deeply, strengthen faith, and rest in what Christ has accomplished.

This is not a schedule to complete. It is a truth to live in.
